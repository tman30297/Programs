# AI & Emerging Tech Trends 2026

## Overview
Research on artificial intelligence, machine learning trends, and emerging technologies as of 2026.

## Key Findings

### Generative AI
- **GPT and Large Language Models** continue to dominate the AI landscape
- Models are getting larger and more capable, but efficiency is becoming equally important
- Multimodal AI (text, image, audio, video) is now standard

### AI Applications
- **Healthcare**: Drug discovery, medical imaging, personalized medicine
- **Finance**: Algorithmic trading, fraud detection, risk assessment
- **Gaming**: Advanced NPCs, procedural generation
- **Agents**: AI agents that can take actions autonomously

### Key Challenges & Ethics
- **Environmental impact**: AI training consumes massive energy
- **Privacy and copyright**: Training data concerns
- **Algorithmic bias**: Fairness issues in AI systems
- **Misinformation**: AI-generated content authenticity
- **Regulatory landscape**: Growing global AI regulations

### Future Directions
- **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)**: Progress toward more general AI systems
- **AI alignment**: Ensuring AI goals align with human values
- **Edge AI**: Running AI on local devices vs cloud
- **Quantum computing**: Potential quantum advantage for AI
