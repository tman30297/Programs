## Part 1: 7 AI Trends to Watch in 2026 (Microsoft)

### 1. AI as a True Partner
- Moving from answering questions to true collaboration
- AI amplifies human capabilities rather than replacing them
- "The future isn't about replacing humans — it's about amplifying them" - Aparna Chennapragada, Microsoft CPO for AI experiences

### 2. AI Agents as Digital Coworkers
- AI agents become teammates, not just tools
- Small teams can punch above their weight (e.g., 3-person team launching global campaigns)
- Humans steer strategy and creativity; AI handles data crunching, content generation, personalization

### 3. Security for AI Agents
- Every agent needs security protections similar to humans
- Give each agent a clear identity, limit access, manage data, protect from threats
- Security becomes ambient, autonomous, and built-in
- "Trust is the currency of innovation"

### 4. AI in Healthcare
- Moving beyond diagnostics into symptom triage and treatment planning
- Microsoft AI's Diagnostic Orchestrator (MAI-DxO) solved complex medical cases with 85.5% accuracy vs 20% for physicians
- Consumer-facing generative AI health products emerging
- Addressing global health worker shortage (11M projected by 2030)

### 5. AI in Scientific Research
- AI generates hypotheses, uses tools to control experiments
- Every researcher could have an AI lab assistant
- Shifting from summarizing papers to active discovery participation

### 6. Smarter AI Infrastructure
- Moving from "bigger datacenters" to efficiency
- Distributed AI "superfactories" drive down costs
- "Measured by quality of intelligence, not sheer size"
- Like air traffic control: dynamic routing ensures every cycle and watt is used

### 7. Repository Intelligence
- AI understands code relationships and history, not just lines
- Analyzes patterns in repositories to understand what changed and why
- Catches errors earlier, automates routine fixes
- "Will become a competitive advantage"

### 8. Quantum + AI Hybrid Computing
- Rise of hybrid computing: quantum + AI + supercomputers
- Microsoft Majorana 1: first quantum chip using topological qubits
- Logical qubits enable error detection and correction
- Path to millions of qubits on a single chip

## Part 2: Agentic AI Frameworks Comparison (2026)

### Market Adoption
- Gartner: 40% of enterprise applications will incorporate AI agents by end of 2026 (up from <5% in 2025)
- McKinsey 2025: 23% of companies scaling agentic AI; 39% actively experimenting

### 5 Core Components of Agentic Frameworks

1. **LLM Reasoning & Planning Engine**
   - ReAct pattern: Reason, Act, Observe
   - Model choices: Claude 4 Opus, GPT-5, Gemini 3 Pro

2. **Tool Use & External Integrations**
   - Web search, code execution, APIs, databases, file operations
   - LangChain leads with 700+ integrations

3. **Agent Memory**
   - Short-term: context within session
   - Long-term: persistent across sessions via vector databases

4. **Orchestration & Multi-Agent Coordination**
   - Hierarchical (manager delegates to workers)
   - Sequential (hand-off in order)
   - Graph-based (nodes with conditional routing)

5. **Human-in-the-Loop Controls**
   - Approval gates, intervention points, audit trails
   - Critical for enterprise deployment

### Framework Selection Guidance

- **Simple, narrow tasks**: OpenAI Assistants API or lightweight options
- **Complex production pipelines**: LangGraph
- **Multi-agent collaboration**: CrewAI (roles) or AutoGen (conversational)
- **Microsoft/Azure stack**: AutoGen + Semantic Kernel (merged)
- **Google/GCP stack**: ADK
- **Data-heavy applications**: LlamaIndex
